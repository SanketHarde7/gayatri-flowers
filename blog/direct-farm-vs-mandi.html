Direct Shirdi Farm Sourcing vs Gultekdi Mandi Brokers

Sourcing Comparison · May 28, 2026 · 6 Min Read

When you are buying wholesale flowers in bulk, you are usually faced with two main options: sifting through rows of brokers at a wholesale flower market (mandi) or partnering with a direct-farm wholesale flower supplier. While mandi markets like Pune's Gultekdi or Mumbai's Dadar look convenient, they hide major costs, high damage rates, and delayed timelines. For event planners looking for the best flower supplier in Pune or a direct-farm flower supplier in Nashik, understanding the path your flowers take from the soil to the stage is critical to maximizing profit margins and ensuring event quality.

In this post, we compare the economics and freshness of sourcing marigolds, roses, and chrysanthemums directly from Shirdi farms vs purchasing from wholesale mandi commission agents, helping you make the right choice for your decoration business.

The Mandi Sourcing Chain (Broker Sourced)

To understand why mandi flowers cost more and dry out faster, look at the complex supply chain route:

  1. Harvest: The grower harvests flowers at their farm.
  2. First Transport: The grower transports flowers to the local mandi.
  3. Mandi Auction: Commission agents auction the flowers to wholesalers. (Middleman markup #1)
  4. Second Transport: Wholesalers move the stock to city-level distribution shops.
  5. Retail Brokerage: Retailers and decorators buy from these wholesalers. (Middleman markup #2)

By the time you purchase the flower basket, it has already been handled 4-5 times, loaded and unloaded multiple times, and spent 24 to 36 hours in transit. This results in bruised petals, dried stems, and a 15% to 20% commission markup that you pay out of pocket. For those sourcing from a traditional flower supplier in Pune or Pune's local mandis, this translates to high rates of wastage (often up to 25% of the total shipment is unusable due to crushing).

The Direct Farm Sourcing Chain (Gayatri Flowers Suppliers)

Direct farm sourcing cuts out every intermediary:

  1. Morning Harvest: Flowers are harvested at 5:00 AM on our Shirdi farm.
  2. Direct Load: Stock is loaded directly onto covered vehicles.
  3. Fast Dispatch: Dispatched immediately to your city (e.g. Pune, Nashik, Ahilyanagar).
  4. Delivery: Arrives at your venue or shop within 2–6 hours of cutting.

No auctions, no middlemen fees, and no overnight storage. You get the lowest farm-direct prices, and your flowers arrive fresh and dew-kissed. That's why decorators searching for the best flower supplier in Nashik or a dependable flower supplier in Ahilyanagar are switching to direct farm accounts.
