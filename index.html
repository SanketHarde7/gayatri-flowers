Swami Chaudhari built Gayatri Flowers Suppliers from the ground up — taking over his family's farm in Shirdi and transforming it into one of Maharashtra's most trusted bulk flower operations. Growing up surrounded by marigold fields and rose beds, he developed a deep understanding of flower quality, seasonal cycles, and what buyers truly need.

Today, Gayatri Flowers is a trademark-registered supplier serving mandap decorators, Blinkit, event planners, and wholesale buyers across Maharashtra. His philosophy is simple: farm honest, supply fresh, service fair.
