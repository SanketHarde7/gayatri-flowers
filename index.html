Frequently Asked

Common questions
from bulk buyers.

Are you a direct farm or a flower reseller?

We are a direct farm owner in Shirdi, Maharashtra. Marigold, rose, and chrysanthemum are grown on our own land — no mandi sourcing, no middlemen. This means fresher flowers, consistent quality, and competitive wholesale pricing for bulk buyers.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum 10 kg for marigold and chrysanthemum. Minimum 5 kg for rose. We supply bulk orders only — not retail or individual bouquet quantities.

How much can you supply daily?

Our daily dispatch capacity is 400–500 kg marigold, 100–150 kg rose, and 200–300 kg chrysanthemum. We can comfortably handle large wedding, event, and wholesale orders without stock issues.

Do you supply flowers outside Shirdi?

Yes. We supply across Maharashtra — Nashik, Pune, Aurangabad, Ahmednagar, Kopargaon, and Sangamner. Large interstate orders to Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh are also accommodated for bulk buyers.

How fresh are the flowers at delivery?

All flowers are harvested the same morning before dispatch from our own farm. We control the entire harvest-to-dispatch timeline — ensuring peak freshness for your event or wholesale order every time.

How do I place a bulk order?

WhatsApp us at +91 93252 84221. Most bulk enquiries receive a rate and availability confirmation within 2 hours, 7 days a week. No complex forms — direct communication with the farm owner.
